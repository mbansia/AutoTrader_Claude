Cross-venue rebalancing

Single-pool framing means capital can (and eventually will) move between venues automatically — today the bot runs same-venue funding arbs only, on Binance (Portfolio Margin) and KuCoin (Classic or UTA). When the cross-venue and onchain orchestrators land, the router will allocate toward whichever venue currently has the best risk-adjusted funding APY (net of fees + transfer cost). The trade-type taxonomy (per-position tags on the Dashboard) is the seam where new venue combinations slot in.
