{# ============= PROFITABILITY GATE ============= #}

Profitability gate

The bot computes net profit per single funding window using the actual fill prices from simulate_fill on both legs and the venue's API-reported taker fee for each symbol (no hard-coded fee assumption). The result is annualized and compared to the entry / exit thresholds in the Strategy thresholds card above — one threshold for "open this fresh now", the other for "keep holding". net_per_window = funding_window − |basis| × (1 + buffer) − 2 × (spot_fee + perp_fee); annualize compounded over the candidate's funding-cycle period.
Worst-case exit basis projection = current basis × this multiple. 3 = assume exit costs 3× the entry/live basis. Round-trip basis cost subtracted from funding = basis × (1 + this).
